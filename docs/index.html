Navigate to my eToro profile (@AndreaRavalli), click the green "Copy" button, choose your allocation amount, and confirm. The platform will automatically replicate all existing and future trades proportionally in real time.

Checking the "Copy Open Trades" box ensures you immediately enter into the full optimized portfolio of 40+ positions at current market prices, rather than waiting for future individual order executions.

While eToro's platform minimum is $200, to achieve precise fractional replication across all 40+ diversified holdings, a starting capital of at least $500 – $1,000 is strongly recommended.

You can add funds to your active copy at any time. Any newly deposited capital is automatically distributed proportionally across all current holdings, executing an effortless Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA) compounding process.
